The Best Movies According to Modather

My top 5 movie franchises of all time.

Lethal Weapon

Two unlikely police officers, Martin Riggs and Roger Murtaugh, have to work as a pair in order to unearth a drug-smuggling operation.

Bad Boys

Bad Boys is an American series of action comedy films starring Will Smith and Martin Lawrence as two detectives in the Miami Police Department, Mike Lowrey and Marcus Burnett. The series was created by George Gallo.

Men in Black

James, an NYC cop, is hired by Agent K of a secret government agency that monitors extraterrestrial life on Earth. Together, they must recover an item that has been stolen by an intergalactic villain.

Beverly Hills Cop

Police officer Axel Foley's close friend Mikey Tandino is murdered soon after he arrives in Detroit. Axel takes up the investigation of the case, which leads him to the crime world of Beverly Hills.

Rush Hour

Two cops from different cultures, who cannot stand each other, team up to save the kidnapped 11-year-old daughter of a diplomat. With time running out, they must nab the criminals and save the girl.
